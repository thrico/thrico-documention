# Leaderboard

## Overview

The **Leaderboard** provides real-time community engagement rankings. It showcases top-performing members ranked by cumulative points, current rank status, and unlocked badges, fostering healthy competition and driving sustained member participation.

---

## Navigation & Access

1. From the dashboard sidebar, navigate to **Gamification** → **Points & Badges**.
2. Click on the **Settings** tab and select **Leaderboard** (`/gamification/points-and-badges/settings/leaderboard`).

## Key Features & Components

### 1. Podium & Top Performers
- **1st Place (Gold Trophy)**: Visual spotlight with gold medal/trophy flair for the community champion.
- **2nd Place (Silver Medal)**: Distinct silver highlighting for the runner-up.
- **3rd Place (Bronze Medal)**: Bronze styling for the third-place finisher.
- **Rank Positions (#4+)**: Clean numeric rank indicators for all subsequent members.

---

### 2. Member Profile Information
Each leaderboard entry displays:
- **Member Identity**: Avatar, full display name, and username/handle.
- **Profile Quick View**: Hover card showing member bio, join date, and membership tier.
- **Current Rank Tier**: Visual badge indicating their status level (e.g., *Gold*, *Diamond*, *Master*).
- **Total Points Earned**: Live total of points accumulated within the selected timeframe.
- **Badges Showroom**: Visual preview of all badges the member has unlocked.

---

## Filters, Timeframes & Sorting

### Timeframe Scopes
Admins and members can view rankings across different intervals:
- **All-Time**: Lifetime cumulative point rankings since joining.
- **Monthly**: Points earned in the current calendar month (refreshes monthly for ongoing competitions).
- **Weekly**: Rolling 7-day engagement leaderboard.
- **Daily / Sprint**: Focused sprint rankings for active community events.

---

### Sorting Options
- **Rank (Ascending / Descending)**: Order from #1 top performer downward.
- **Points (Highest to Lowest)**: View highest score totals.
- **Badges Count**: Sort members by the number of unique badges unlocked.

---

## View Modes

### Table View
A comprehensive data table displaying:
- **Rank**: Position medal / rank number.
- **Member**: User avatar, name, and profile link.
- **Current Rank Tier**: Metallic/gem rank badge.
- **Points Balance**: Total score with currency/point icon.
- **Badges Earned**: Mini icon tray of unlocked achievements.
- **Actions (`...`)**: View full member profile, copy user ID, or inspect event history.

### Grid / Card View
Visual cards tailored for showcase presentations, featuring large member avatars, prominent rank emblems, badge displays, and score counters.

---

## Administrative Actions

- **Search Member**: Instant debounced search by name, email, or handle.
- **Export Data**: Download current leaderboard standings and scores in **CSV** or **JSON** format for external rewards, audits, or community shoutouts.
- **Inspect Member History**: Click through to the **User History** tab to view the detailed point transaction log for any specific member.
