# Engagement Games

## Overview

**Engagement Games** give members fun, interactive ways to win points, digital currencies, discount coupons, and exclusive badges. Administrators have granular control over entry costs, win probabilities, prize pools, and cooldown limits.

---

## Supported Game Types

### 🎡 1. Spin the Wheel
A classic fortune wheel game where members spend energy or credits to spin for prizes.
- **Configurable Slices**: Set between 6 to 12 slices with custom labels, icons, and prize weights.
- **Guaranteed vs Chance Odds**: Control weighted probabilities for each slice (e.g., 50% small points, 5% jackpot voucher, 15% try again).
- **Visual Themes**: Choose between arcade, neon, casino, or minimal aesthetic styles.

---

### 🎟️ 2. Scratch Cards
A virtual scratch-off card experience where members reveal hidden symbols.
- **Match Requirement**: Uncover 3 identical icons to win the corresponding prize tier.
- **Dynamic Reveal Animations**: Interactive touch/cursor scratch interaction.
- **Mystery Jackpots**: Ability to seed rare mystery reward codes into daily batches.

---

### 🃏 3. Match & Win
A memory tile-matching game where members uncover pairs of cards under a countdown timer.
- **Grid Layouts**: 2x2 (easy), 3x4 (medium), or 4x4 (advanced) card grids.
- **Time Attack**: Members must finish within the timer to qualify for tier rewards.
- **Score Multipliers**: Bonus points awarded for fewer moves and faster completion times.

---

## Game Administration & Economics

### Setting Entry Requirements & Cooldowns
- **Free Daily Plays**: Give members `N` free plays per calendar day.
- **Energy Cost**: Require energy units (e.g., 20 ⚡ per play) once free turns are consumed.
- **Currency Buy-In**: Allow members to wager virtual coins for entry into high-stakes reward rounds.
- **Cooldown Interval**: Enforce waiting times between attempts (e.g., 1 play every 4 hours).
