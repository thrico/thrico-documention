## Overview

## Core Sub-Modules

### 🪙 Points & Badges
Award points for core community actions (posts, comments, event attendance) and third-party integrations (Shopify purchases, Discord activity). Unlock collectible achievement badges and climb rank tiers.

### 🏅 Leaderboards
Real-time community rankings with podium champion highlights, dynamic timeframe intervals (All-Time, Monthly, Weekly, Sprint), and member showcase cards.

### 💳 Currency & Wallets
Manage digital community currencies, member wallets, real-time balance tracking, transaction audit logs, and economic inflation controls.

### ⚡ Energy & Streaks
Implement energy recharge mechanics where members spend energy units to play games or unlock exclusive interactions, encouraging daily recurring check-ins with streak multipliers.

### 🎮 Engagement Games
Interactive gamified experiences including **Spin the Wheel**, **Scratch Cards**, and **Match & Win** with customizable reward pools, winning probabilities, and daily cooldowns.

### 🎁 Rewards & Store
Redeemable rewards catalog featuring discount coupons, digital vouchers, physical merchandise, and exclusive perks with built-in fraud prevention and inventory tracking.

### 🌐 Impact Score Engine
An algorithmic reputation and influence system evaluating member participation quality across 5 core pillars: Engagement, Contribution, Trust & Safety, Network, and Consistency.
