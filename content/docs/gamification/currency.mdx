# Virtual Currency & Economy

## Overview & System Purpose

The **Virtual Currency Subsystem** (`/gamification/currency/*`) manages the internal tokenomics of your community ecosystem. It enables administrators to define custom branded currency (e.g., *Coins, Gems, Credits, Tokens*), set conversion rates between raw gamification points and spendable currency, implement anti-fraud guardrails, and audit member wallets and ledgers.

## Economic Configuration (`/economics`)

### Custom Branding
- **Currency Name**: Choose a custom name that fits your brand identity (e.g., *Credits, Coins, Gems, Stars, Tokens, Points*).
- **Display Icon & Symbol**: Visual asset representing your digital currency in user wallets, rewards stores, and leaderboards.

### Normalization & Conversion Ratio
- **Normalization Factor**: Defines the exchange rate from raw gamification points to spendable currency tokens.
  - *Example*: A normalization factor of `100` means **100 Points = 1 Currency Coin**.
- **Conversion Presets**: Quick selectors for ratios (`1:1`, `10:1`, `50:1`, `100:1`, `500:1`).
- **Live Simulator**: Built-in interactive calculator to test how raw points convert to spendable currency before saving changes.

## Risk Management & Anti-Abuse (`/risk`)

Prevent runaway inflation, bot exploitation, and economic abuse by enforcing automated guardrails:

| Guardrail Setting | Description & Protection |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Max Currency Per Member / Day** | The maximum allowable currency a single member can accumulate within 24 hours. |
| **Max Currency Per Member / Month** | The monthly accumulation cap preventing outlier exploitation. |
| **Max Global Entity Reserve** | Total ecosystem ceiling on circulating currency supply. |
| **Redemption Rate Limit** | Frequency throttling on reward shop redemptions. |

## Member Wallet Directory (`/member-wallet`)

The **Member Wallet** dashboard provides visibility into individual accounts across the platform:

- **Search & Filtering**: Search members by name, email, or wallet ID.
- **Balance Insights**:
  - **Current Spendable Balance**: Real-time balance available for rewards and redemptions.
  - **Lifetime Earned**: Total historical currency minted for the member.
  - **Lifetime Spent / Burned**: Total currency exchanged in the rewards marketplace.
- **Transaction Ledger Modal**:
  - Click the **History** icon on any member row to open the complete itemized transaction ledger.
  - Displays timestamps, source actions, delta (`+` or `-`), transaction IDs, and reference triggers.

## Quick Trace & Auditing (`/trace` & `/audit-log`)

### Quick Trace (`/trace`)
- Search any transaction ID, event hash, or user ID to instantly trace:
  - Timestamp of event execution.
  - Triggering action and origin module.
  - Conversion rate applied at the time of the transaction.
  - Pre-transaction balance vs post-transaction balance.

### Audit Log (`/audit-log`)
- Complete compliance log capturing:
  - Changes to currency name or conversion factors.
  - Modifications to risk caps and daily limits.
  - Administrative manual adjustments or re-seed operations.
