# Energy & Streak Mechanics

## Overview

The **Energy System** introduces a replenishable resource model commonly used in gamified applications. Members consume energy units to play mini-games, perform high-impact community actions, or unlock special perks. Energy naturally regenerates over time, driving high-frequency daily retention.

---

## Energy Mechanics

```
  ┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
  │                 Daily Energy Lifecycle                 │
  └──────────────────────────┬─────────────────────────────┘
                             │
                             ▼
              ⚡ Full Energy Pool (e.g. 100 ⚡)
                             │
            ┌────────────────┴────────────────┐
            ▼                                 ▼
   🎮 Play Mini-Games                ⭐ High-Value Actions
   (Cost: 20 ⚡ per spin)             (Cost: 10 ⚡ per entry)
            │                                 │
            └────────────────┬────────────────┘
                             ▼
              🔋 Depleted Energy Pool
                             │
                             ▼
        ⏳ Natural Regeneration (e.g. +10 ⚡ / hour)
                  or Instant Daily Reset
```

### Energy Parameters

| Configuration | Description | Default Value |
|---------------|-------------|---------------|
| **Max Energy Pool** | Maximum energy capacity a member can store at once. | `100 ⚡` |
| **Recharge Rate** | Units regenerated automatically per fixed time window. | `+10 ⚡ / hour` |
| **Daily Reset Hour** | Optional hard reset to 100% capacity at a specific UTC time. | `00:00 UTC` |
| **Energy Boost Items** | Consumable items that instantly refill the energy bar. | Enabled |

## Daily Streaks & Multipliers

Streaks reward members for consecutive days of activity within the community.

### Streak Progression Ladders
- **Day 1–3**: 1.0x baseline point multiplier.
- **Day 4–7**: 1.25x point boost + 10 bonus energy on check-in.
- **Day 8–14**: 1.5x point boost + exclusive badge unlock.
- **Day 30+**: 2.0x legendary multiplier + VIP community flair.

### Streak Recovery & Freeze
- **Streak Freeze Shields**: Members can hold or purchase freeze shields that protect their streak from resetting if they miss a single day.
- **Grace Period**: Configurable 6-hour grace window before a broken streak is reset to zero.
