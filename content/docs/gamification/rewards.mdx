# Rewards & Redemptions

## Overview

The **Rewards Store** is where community members convert their hard-earned points, currencies, or rank achievements into tangible value. Administrators can list digital discount coupons, gift vouchers, exclusive merchandise, or one-on-one experiences.

---

## Reward Types

### 🎟️ 1. Discount Coupons & Promo Codes
- **Dynamic Code Pools**: Upload CSV batches of unique, single-use Shopify/WooCommerce coupon codes.
- **Fixed Discount or Percentage**: Configurable discount percentages (e.g., 20% off) or fixed currency reductions (e.g., $10 off).
- **Minimum Cart Requirements**: Synchronize cart thresholds with integrated storefronts.

---

### 🎁 2. Digital Vouchers & Gift Cards
- **Instant Code Delivery**: Delivered immediately to the member's in-app reward vault and sent via confirmation email.
- **Third-Party Integrations**: Issue Amazon, Apple, Google Play, or custom vendor gift codes.

---

### 📦 3. Physical Merchandise & Perks
- **Inventory Tracking**: Set stock counts; rewards automatically display as "Sold Out" once depleted.
- **Shipping Address Capture**: Prompt members for shipping information during redemption.
- **Fulfillment Pipeline**: Track orders through `Pending` → `Processing` → `Shipped` → `Delivered` statuses.

---

## Setting Up a Reward Item

### Step 1: Basic Information
- **Title & Description**: Engaging headline, product description, terms, and redemption instructions.
- **Visual Media**: High-resolution product images or badge artwork.
- **Category**: Classify under *Discounts*, *Digital Goods*, *VIP Access*, or *Merch*.

---

### Step 2: Pricing & Eligibility
- **Point / Currency Cost**: Specify the exact points or coins needed to claim (e.g., `500 Points` or `50 Coins`).
- **Required Minimum Rank**: Restrict redemption eligibility to specific rank tiers (e.g., only *Gold* members and above).
- **Per-User Limits**: Limit claims per user (e.g., max 1 per member per month).

---

## Redemptions & Fraud Prevention

- **Cooldown Timers**: Prevent rapid sequential claims from automated bots.
- **Approval Queues**: Option to require manual administrator approval for high-value redemptions prior to code issuance.
- **Cancellation & Refunds**: Return spent points/coins back to member wallets if an order cannot be fulfilled.
