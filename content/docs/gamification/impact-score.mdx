# Impact Score Engine

## Overview & Philosophy

The **Impact Score Engine** is an algorithmic reputation and influence system designed to evaluate genuine member value and participation quality within your community. 

Unlike traditional points (which measure simple quantity), the **Impact Score** combines multi-category contributions, trust levels, network activity, consistency, and decay algorithms to produce a holistic score for each member.

## The 5 Core Impact Categories

Every action and rule in the Impact Engine is categorized into one of five standard pillars:

| Category | Icon / Focus | Typical Triggers & Actions |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Engagement** | 💬 *Discussions & Interaction* | Forum replies, chat participation, upvoting, reacting to content. |
| **Contribution** | 🔥 *High-Value Creation* | Publishing articles, authoring guides, answering questions, verified solutions. |
| **Trust & Safety** | 🛡️ *Reputation & Moderation* | ID verification, peer vouches, moderation assistance, zero infraction history. |
| **Network & Referrals**| 👥 *Community Growth* | Successful member invites, follower milestones, peer mentorships. |
| **Consistency** | 🧭 *Retention & Habit* | Daily login streaks, regular weekly event attendance, recurring visits. |

## Creating & Editing Impact Rules

### Step 1: Navigate to Rules
1. Go to **Gamification** → **Impact Score** → **Rules**.
2. Click **Create Rule** (`/gamification/impact-score/rules/create`).

### Step 2: Configure Rule Parameters
- **Source & Origin**:
  - Select between **Core Module** (Community, Courses, Events, Store) or **Integration** (Shopify, Discord, Slack, CRM).
- **Trigger Event / Action**:
  - Select the specific event trigger (e.g., `post_accepted_answer`, `article_published`, `invite_converted`).
- **Category Assignment**:
  - Assign to one of the 5 Impact Categories (*Engagement, Contribution, Trust, Network, Consistency*).
- **Impact Point Weighting**:
  - Set the score delta. Supports both **positive** rewards (e.g., `+25`, `+100`) and **negative** penalties (e.g., `-50` for moderation flags), ranging from `-1000` to `+1000`.
- **Dynamic Calculation Formula (Optional)**:
  - Apply custom formulas or multipliers based on event metadata (e.g., `base * word_count_factor` or `likes * 1.5`).
- **Daily Cap / Frequency Limits**:
  - Set the maximum impact points a user can accumulate from this specific rule within a single day.
- **Description**:
  - Provide an internal explanation for administrator reference.

### Step 3: Save & Activate
Click **Save Rule** in the bottom floating panel. The rule immediately applies to new incoming events.

## Member Impact Directory

The **Member Scores** section provides complete transparency into community member rankings:
- **Impact Score**: Total calculated impact rating.
- **Category Breakdown Radar/Bars**: Percentage contribution from each of the 5 categories (e.g., 40% Contribution, 30% Engagement, 20% Trust, 10% Consistency).
- **Trend Indicator**: Upward or downward score trajectory over 7, 30, and 90 days.
- **Member Inspection**: Click any member to inspect their complete transaction and scoring history.

## Activity & Audit Logs

### Activity Log
- Live event stream recording every member action that triggered an impact rule.
- Displays Timestamp, Member, Action, Module, Points Delta (`+` or `-`), and Category tag.
- Searchable by user handle, module, or date range.

### Audit Log
- Administrative record tracking changes made to impact rules, templates, and formulas.
- Details Admin Name, Modified Rule/Setting, Old Value vs New Value, and Timestamp to ensure governance.

---

## Templates & Algorithm Settings

- **Active Template**: Switch between predefined scoring models (e.g., *Community Heavy*, *Creator Focused*, *Learning First*).
- **Score Decay (Inactivity Penalty)**: Optional periodic decay model where inactive members gradually lose non-permanent engagement scores, keeping leaderboards fresh and dynamic.
- **Score Normalization**: Normalize raw points onto a standard 0–1,000 or 0–100 scale for consistent presentation across platforms.
