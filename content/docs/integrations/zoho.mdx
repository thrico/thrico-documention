The Thrico-Zoho CRM integration connects your Zoho CRM contacts, leads, accounts, and custom modules directly with your Thrico community directory and engagement workflows.

---

## 🌟 Overview

Synchronizing Zoho CRM with Thrico enables automatic member provisioning, contact profile enrichment, and community space auto-assignment.

### Key Capabilities:
- 🔄 **Multi-Region Support**: Seamless connection for Global (`.com`), India (`.in`), Europe (`.eu`), Australia (`.com.au`), Japan (`.jp`), and Canada (`.ca`) Zoho data centers.
- 🏷️ **Dynamic Module & Field Discovery**: Automatically discovers standard modules (`Contacts`, `Leads`, `Accounts`) and custom fields using Zoho V8 API.
- ⚡ **Auto-Token Refresh**: Long-lived offline refresh tokens ensure continuous background sync without re-authentication.
- 🛡️ **Zero-Code Setup**: One-click authorization through the Thrico Admin Dashboard.

---

## 📋 Prerequisites

1. A **Zoho CRM account** (Standard, Professional, Enterprise, or Ultimate) with Administrator permissions.
2. Access to the **Zoho Developer Console** ([api-console.zoho.com](https://api-console.zoho.com) or your local region).
3. Access to your **Thrico Admin Dashboard** (`https://admin.thrico.app`).

## 🚀 Step-by-Step Connection Guide

<Steps>
<Step>
### Step 1: Open the Zoho API Console

Navigate to the Zoho API Console corresponding to your organization's data center region:

- 🌐 **Global / US**: [api-console.zoho.com](https://api-console.zoho.com/)
- 🇮🇳 **India**: [api-console.zoho.in](https://api-console.zoho.in/)
- 🇪🇺 **Europe**: [api-console.zoho.eu](https://api-console.zoho.eu/)
- 🇦🇺 **Australia**: [api-console.zoho.com.au](https://api-console.zoho.com.au/)
- 🇯🇵 **Japan**: [api-console.zoho.jp](https://api-console.zoho.jp/)
- 🇨🇦 **Canada**: [api-console.zoho.ca](https://api-console.zoho.ca/)
</Step>

<Step>
### Step 2: Create a Server-Based Client Application

1. In the Zoho API Console, click **`Add Client`** (or **`Get Started`**).
2. Hover over **Server-based Applications** and click **`Create Now`**.
3. Fill in the application registration details:
   - **Client Name**: `Thrico CRM Integration`
   - **Homepage URL**: `https://admin.thrico.app`
   - **Authorized Redirect URIs**: Enter your Thrico OAuth callback URL:

<Tabs items={["Production (admin.thrico.app)", "Development / Local"]}>
  <Tab value="Production (admin.thrico.app)">
```text
https://admin.thrico.app/integrations/crm/callback
```
  </Tab>
  <Tab value="Development / Local">
```text
http://localhost:1111/integrations/crm/callback
```
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

4. Click **`Create`**.
</Step>

<Step>
### Step 3: Copy Client ID & Client Secret

1. Switch to the **Client Secret** tab on your newly created Zoho application.
2. Copy the two credentials:
   - 🔑 **Client ID** (e.g., `1000.TVBB9121HP9...`)
   - 🔒 **Client Secret** (e.g., `4e8b39a...`)

<Callout type="warn">
  Keep your Client Secret confidential. Thrico securely encrypts and stores all credentials at rest using AES-256 GCM.
</Callout>
</Step>

<Step>
### Step 4: Authorize from the Thrico Dashboard

1. Open your **Thrico Dashboard** &rarr; navigate to:
   ```text
   https://admin.thrico.app/settings/integrations/crm
   ```
2. Locate the **Zoho CRM** card and click **`Connect Zoho`**.
3. Select your Zoho data center region (e.g., *India (.in)*, *Global (.com)*, or *Europe (.eu)*).
4. Sign in to Zoho and click **`Accept`** on the OAuth permissions consent screen.
5. You will be automatically redirected back to Thrico with status **CONNECTED**.
</Step>
</Steps>

## ⚙️ Post-Connection Setup

### 1. Module & Field Mapping
- Navigate to the **Field Mapping** tab:
  - Select your primary sync object (**`Contacts`** or **`Leads`**).
  - Map Zoho fields (`First_Name`, `Last_Name`, `Email`, `Phone`, `Account_Name`, custom picklists) to Thrico member profile fields.

### 2. Initial Data Import
- On the **Sync & History** tab, click **`⚡ Sync Now`** to trigger a full bulk import of existing Zoho contacts into your Thrico community.

### 3. Real-Time Webhook Setup (Optional)
To stream new Zoho contacts instantaneously into Thrico:
1. In Zoho CRM &rarr; **Setup (⚙️) &rarr; Automation &rarr; Workflow Rules**.
2. Click **Create Rule**:
   - **Module**: `Contacts`
   - **When**: *A record is Created or Edited*
3. Under **Instant Actions**, choose **Webhook**:
   - **URL**: `POST https://admin.thrico.app/integrations/crm/events`
   - **Method**: `POST`
4. Click **Save & Associate**.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

<Accordions>
<Accordion title="How do I resolve 'invalid_client' during Zoho connection?">
Make sure the <b>Client ID</b> and <b>Client Secret</b> match the exact Zoho Data Center region (e.g. <code>api-console.zoho.in</code> vs <code>api-console.zoho.com</code>) where your Zoho CRM account was registered.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Does Thrico handle Zoho access token expiration?">
Yes. Zoho access tokens expire after 1 hour. Thrico securely stores the <code>refresh_token</code> and automatically acquires fresh access tokens in the background with zero disruption.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I sync custom Zoho modules?">
Yes. Thrico queries the Zoho V8 metadata endpoint to automatically discover all standard and custom modules created in your Zoho organization.
</Accordion>
</Accordions>
