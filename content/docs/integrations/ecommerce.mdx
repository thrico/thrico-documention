## Supported E-Commerce Platforms

<Tabs items={["Shopify", "WooCommerce"]}>
<Tab value="Shopify">
### Shopify Integration

Thrico supports official Shopify App connections with OAuth and webhook synchronization:

1. In your **Thrico Dashboard**, go to **Settings &rarr; Integrations &rarr; E-Commerce**.
2. Click **Connect Shopify**.
3. Enter your myshopify store URL (e.g. `your-brand.myshopify.com`).
4. Approve the required scopes:
   - `read_products`, `read_orders`, `read_customers`, `read_inventory`
5. Thrico automatically subscribes to Shopify webhooks:
   - `customers/create`, `customers/update`
   - `orders/create`, `orders/paid`, `orders/fulfilled`
   - `products/create`, `products/update`, `products/delete`
   - `inventory_levels/update`

</Tab>
<Tab value="WooCommerce">
### WooCommerce Integration

Connect your WordPress WooCommerce store using REST API keys:

1. In WordPress Admin, navigate to **WooCommerce &rarr; Settings &rarr; Advanced &rarr; REST API**.
2. Click **Add Key**, set description to `Thrico Integration`, and permissions to `Read/Write`.
3. Copy the **Consumer Key** and **Consumer Secret**.
4. In Thrico Dashboard:
   - Enter your WordPress site URL: `https://yourstore.com`
   - Enter `Consumer Key` and `Consumer Secret`.
5. Webhooks are automatically configured for order and product synchronization.

</Tab>
</Tabs>

## Features & Benefits

### 1. Automatic Customer Community Onboarding
- Invite store customers into your community immediately after checkout.
- Reward repeat buyers with exclusive community access and badges.

### 2. Product Catalog Synchronization
- Import products and collections directly into the Thrico Marketplace.
- Keep pricing, variants, images, and inventory in sync automatically.

### 3. Queue-Based Processing & Fault Tolerance
All incoming Shopify and WooCommerce webhook events are published to dedicated RabbitMQ queues (`shopify.customer.sync`, `shopify.order.sync`, `woocommerce.order.sync`). In the event of network timeouts, jobs route to `shopify.dlq` or `woocommerce.dlq` for safe replay without data loss.
