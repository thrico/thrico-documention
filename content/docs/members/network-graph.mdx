# Interactive Network Relationship Graph

## Overview

The **Network Graph** (`/members/graph`) is a visual intelligence and analytics tool built with interactive D3 force-directed simulations. It models the social and operational fabric of your community, turning raw engagement logs into intuitive network relationship maps.

---

## Graph Modeling & Semantics

```
              (Member A)
             /    │     \
            /     │      \ (Mentorship)
   (Referral)  (Co-Member) \
          /       │          \
         ▼        ▼           ▼
    (Member B)---(Group)---(Member C)
```

### Nodes
- **Members**: Colored by membership tier or classification chapter. Size scales proportionally with community Impact Score or point balance.
- **Entities / Groups**: Central anchors linking co-members across shared channels and events.

### Edges (Relationships)
- **Direct Friendships / Connections**: Mutual peer connections.
- **Referral Bonds**: Directed arrows from inviter to invitee.
- **Co-Attendance**: Connections formed by attending identical workshops or webinars.
- **Mentorship Ties**: Verified mentor-mentee linkages.

---

## Interactive Controls & Features

### 1. Dynamic Physics Simulation
- Drag nodes to reposition clusters in real-time.
- Adjust charge force, link distance, and collision radius to expand dense clusters.

### 2. Multi-Layer Filters
- **Filter by Tier**: Isolate and examine interactions among VIPs or founding members.
- **Filter by Classification**: Highlight specific cohorts (e.g., *Class of 2026*) or chapters.
- **Edge Weight Threshold**: Only render strong ties with multiple interactions.

### 3. Cluster & Influence Hub Detection
- Automatically identifies dense micro-communities and cross-pollinating members who bridge disconnected groups.
- Pinpoints isolated members who may require targeted onboarding outreach.

### 4. Node Inspection Modal
- Clicking any node opens an instant slide-out tray displaying the member's profile summary, key stats, direct connection count, and quick messaging options.
