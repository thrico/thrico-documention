# Referral Program & Virality Engine

## Overview

The **Referral Subsystem** (`/members/referrals`) drives organic community growth by empowering members to invite peers through personal referral links and rewarded viral loops.

---

## Key Capabilities

### 1. Personalized Referral Links
- Every member receives a unique referral code and short tracking URL upon account creation.
- Deep-linking support to specific events, courses, or sub-communities.

---

### 2. Multi-Stage Attribution & Tracking
Track every stage of the referral funnel in real-time:
- **Link Clicks**: Volume of traffic driven by the referrer.
- **Account Signups**: New users who registered using the link.
- **Activation / Verification**: Referrals who completed their profile and email verification.
- **Tier Conversions**: Referred members who upgraded to a paid membership tier.

---

### 3. Inviter-Invitee Relationship Trees
Visual hierarchy mapping the complete lineage of invitations:
- View multi-tier referral networks (Direct Referrals vs Secondary Downline Referrals).
- Trace virality coefficients and identify top platform evangelists.

---

### 4. Automated Referral Rewards
Configure automatic triggers when a referred member completes key milestones:
- **On Signup**: Award 50 Points to both inviter and invitee.
- **On First Event Attendance**: Grant a "Community Ambassador" badge.
- **On Paid Tier Upgrade**: Credit a percentage of first-month subscription fees in currency coins.

---

## Referral Leaderboard & Insights
- **Top Referrers Table**: Real-time rankings of members by total confirmed conversions.
- **Campaign Filtering**: Segment referrals by seasonal marketing pushes or custom UTM tags.
- **Fraud Prevention**: Detection of self-referrals, disposable emails, and abnormal registration velocity from identical IP subnets.
