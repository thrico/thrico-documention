# Governance, Moderation & Audit

## Overview

The **Governance Suite** ensures compliance, platform safety, and institutional accountability across the member directory through moderation queues, audit logs, and global policy configurations.

---

## 1. Reported Members Queue (`/members/reports`)

When community members or automated AI moderation filters flag a profile, it enters the **Reports Triage Queue**:

- **Report Details**: Shows reporter identity, reported member, timestamp, violation reason category (*Spam, Harassment, Inappropriate Content, Impersonation*), and context evidence.
- **Triage Actions**:
  - **Dismiss Report**: Mark as reviewed with no infraction recorded.
  - **Issue Warning**: Send a formal automated warning notification to the member.
  - **Temporary Suspension**: Place the account on a 24-hour, 7-day, or 30-day freeze.
  - **Permanent Ban & Archive**: Expel the member and purge active sessions across web and mobile.

---

## 2. Immutable Member Audit Log (`/members/audit-log`)

Every administrative action affecting member accounts is captured in an append-only audit trail:

- **Recorded Events**:
  - Manual tier promotions or demotions.
  - Role modifications (e.g., granting Moderator permissions).
  - Manual point and currency adjustments.
  - Profile attribute edits executed by staff.
  - Account activations, suspensions, and data export requests.
- **Metadata Captured**: Admin user ID, IP address, timestamp, target member ID, and pre-modification vs post-modification state diff.

---

## 3. Global Member Settings (`/members/settings`)

- **Profile Schema Builder**: Add custom text, dropdown, URL, date, or multi-select fields to member profiles.
- **Privacy Defaults**: Control whether member directory listings, email addresses, and phone numbers are visible publicly, to logged-in members only, or strictly to administrators.
- **Onboarding Requirements**: Set mandatory profile completion criteria (e.g., must upload an avatar and fill out bio before posting in feeds).
- **GDPR & Right-to-be-Forgotten**: Execute automated account anonymization and data export packages on demand.
