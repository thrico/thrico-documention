# Membership Tiers

## Overview

The **Membership Tiers Engine** (`/members/tiers`) enables communities to structure access levels, monetize premium offerings, and recognize distinguished contributors. Tiers define paywalls, exclusive content visibility, special event access, and recurring automated perks.

---

## Tier Structure & Hierarchy

Organizations can define multiple ascending tier levels:

```
  ┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
  │                 Tier Progression Hierarchy               │
  └────────────────────────────┬─────────────────────────────┘
                               │
            ┌──────────────────┼──────────────────┐
            ▼                  ▼                  ▼
       🥉 Free Member      🥈 Pro Member     🥇 Executive VIP
       • Public Feed       • Premium Groups   • All Groups
       • Basic Events      • Exclusive Events • VIP Concierge
       • Standard Badges   • Monthly Bonus    • 2x Point Boost
```

---

## Tier Configuration Settings

### Basic Identity
- **Tier Name & Slug**: Descriptive name (e.g., *Community Pioneer, VIP Patron*).
- **Badge & Color Theme**: Metallic/gem accent flair displayed on member avatars and directory listings.
- **Description**: Public summary of tier benefits and eligibility rules.

---

### Access Control & Gating
- **Community Gating**: Specify which sub-communities, discussion boards, and feed channels are unlocked for this tier.
- **Course & Content Access**: Grant full access or discounts on premium courses and digital resources.
- **Event Ticketing**: Offer free RSVPs, early-bird registration windows, or exclusive VIP ticket tiers.

---

### Automated Tier Perks
- **Monthly Point / Coin Stipend**: Automatically credit a recurring balance of points or currency tokens on the first of each month.
- **Earning Multipliers**: Apply a global point boost (e.g., 1.5x on all actions) while the member maintains this tier.
- **Priority Moderation**: Priority queue for verified forum answers and marketplace listings.

---

## Billing & Payment Gateway Integration

Connect with Stripe or Shopify to support paid membership tiers:
- **Recurring Subscriptions**: Monthly, quarterly, or annual billing cycles.
- **Free Trials & Grace Periods**: Automated grace periods for failed card payments before tier downgrade.
- **Automatic Downgrade / Upgrade Webhooks**: Instant synchronization of access status upon subscription changes.
