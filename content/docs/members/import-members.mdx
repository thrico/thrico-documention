## Overview

This guide walks you through how to **import members** in Thrico.

## Prerequisites

- **Entity Admin** or **Community Admin** role
- At least one active community

## Navigation

```
Dashboard → Members → Import Members
```

## Step-by-Step Instructions

### Step 1: Navigate to Members

Open the **Members** section from the sidebar.

### Step 2: Follow the Workflow

Complete the required steps for import members.

### Step 3: Confirm

Review and confirm your actions.
