# Gamification & Impact Engine Exclusions

## Overview

The **Gamification & Impact Engine Exclusion Controls** allow community managers and administrators to selectively disable automated gamification rewards (points, streaks, and badges) and algorithmic impact score calculations for individual member accounts.

This ensures internal staff, community moderators, integration test bots, and VIP profiles do not unfairly dominate leaderboards or distort engagement analytics.

---

## Key Use Cases

- **Staff & Team Accounts**: Internal staff who publish announcements, reply to tickets, or moderate posts on a daily basis can be excluded to avoid accumulating disproportionate points.
- **Bot & Automation Accounts**: System users, webhook integrations, and test accounts can be prevented from skewing leaderboard standings.
- **Fair Community Leaderboards**: Ensures monthly member prize pools and public competitions reflect genuine member activity rather than administrative actions.
- **Audited Compliance**: All changes to exclusion statuses are tracked in the audit trail with the responsible administrator's details and timestamps.

---

## Exclusion Controls Breakdown

Administrators can toggle two independent controls located in the **Edit Member** form:

| Control | Field Key | Default | Impact When Enabled (`ON`) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Exclude from Gamification Points** | `excludeFromGamification` | `OFF` (`false`) | Halts automated point awards, activity streaks, level advancement, and badge earnings across all platform events. |
| **Exclude from Impact Score Calculation** | `excludeFromImpactScore` | `OFF` (`false`) | Excludes member engagement events from the algorithmic Impact Score pipeline and leaderboards. |

> **Preservation of Existing Data:** Enabling an exclusion does **not** delete previously earned badges, transaction logs, or historical points. It only halts future automated calculation and reward jobs.

## Step-by-Step Operator Guide

### Step 1: Locate the Member Record
1. From the main sidebar, navigate to **Members** (`/members`).
2. Search for the member by name, username, or email address.
3. In the member table or grid, click the action menu (**•••**) and select **Edit Member**.
   - *Direct Route:* `/members/[id]/edit`

---

### Step 2: Configure Gamification & Impact Controls
1. On the **Edit Member** screen, scroll down to the **Gamification & Impact Engine Controls** card.
2. Toggle the switches according to your operational policy:
   - 🏆 **Exclude from Gamification Points**: Flip to `ON` to pause points and badge accrual.
   - 📈 **Exclude from Impact Score Calculation**: Flip to `ON` to pause algorithmic impact tracking.
3. Click **Save Changes** in the bottom-right action dock.

---

### Step 3: Verify the Member's Status
1. Navigate to the member's 360° profile console at `/members/[id]?tab=gamification`.
2. When exclusions are active, an amber status banner appears above the summary cards:
   > ⚠️ **Calculations Excluded / Paused**  
   > *This member is currently excluded from automated Gamification point awards and/or Impact Score calculations.*

---

## Audit Trail & Security Logs

Every update to member gamification exclusion settings generates an immutable record in the **Audit Logs**:

- **Location**: `/members/audit-log`
- **Action Type**: `UPDATE_MEMBER`
- **Module**: `USERS`
- **Logged Details**: Includes the responsible admin's name and email, timestamp, and the previous vs. updated exclusion states.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Can an excluded member still use the community normally?
**Yes.** Excluded members experience zero disruptions. They can browse, post, comment, message, and attend events normally. The engine simply skips awarding points or recalculating their impact metrics in background worker queues.

### Can exclusions be reversed?
**Yes.** Return to `/members/[id]/edit`, switch the toggles back to **OFF**, and click **Save Changes**. The member will immediately resume participating in gamification and impact score updates for new events.
