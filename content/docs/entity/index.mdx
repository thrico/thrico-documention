# Entity Management & Settings

## What is an Entity?

An **Entity** in Thrico represents an organization, institution, or business that uses the platform. Entities are the top-level organizational unit and contain communities, members, and all associated data.

---

## Key Capabilities

- **Registration & Setup** — Create new entities with custom configurations
- **Branding** — Customize logo, colors, fonts, and visual identity
- **Custom Domains** — Connect your own domain with automated TLS/SSL provisioning
- **Module Management** — Enable, disable, rename, and reorder Web and Mobile tabs
- **Packages & Features** — Assign feature packages and quotas
- **Billing & Subscriptions** — Manage platform subscriptions and payment methods

---

## Entity Lifecycle

```
Register → Configure → Brand → Connect Domain → Setup Modules → Launch → Scale
```
