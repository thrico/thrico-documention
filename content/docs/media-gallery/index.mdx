## Overview

The **Thrico Media Gallery** is a visual storytelling platform for communities, alumni networks, and enterprise ecosystems. It enables administrators and members to curate albums, share high-resolution photos, upload video reels with automated cloud transcoding, and engage through threaded comments.

With the **Headless Developer SDK**, external websites, event portals, and mobile apps can embed turnkey photo/video upload widgets and programmatic APIs with built-in daily quota limits and content moderation.

---

## Key Capabilities

- **Curated Albums** — Organize media by events, graduation years, chapters, or special projects.
- **Photos & Native Video Reels** — Multi-format media support with automated S3 chunked uploads and HLS video streaming.
- **Third-Party Upload SDK & Embed Widgets** — Let attendees, students, or partners upload media directly from external event landing pages.
- **Daily Upload Quotas** — Enforce per-uploader and community-wide daily limits to prevent spam and storage abuse.
- **Content Moderation Queue** — Review, approve, or reject third-party submissions before they appear publicly in albums.
- **Community Feed Integration** — Automatically feature album highlights directly in the central feed and allow social sharing.
