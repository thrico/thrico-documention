---

## Overview

Albums allow administrators and community leads to organize photos and videos into structured collections such as Events, Graduating Batches, Milestones, and Spotlights.

## Features

- **Cover Image & Badges** — Set high-resolution cover photos for each album.
- **Featured Albums** — Pin priority albums to the top of the community gallery.
- **Ordering & Sorting** — Custom ordering of images and videos.
- **Feed Syndication** — Display album updates directly in the community activity feed.
