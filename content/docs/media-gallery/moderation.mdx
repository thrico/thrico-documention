---
title: Media Moderation Queue
description: Review and approve photos and videos submitted via external SDKs and embed widgets.
---

## Overview

When third-party uploads are permitted with **Require Moderation** enabled, all incoming photos and videos enter the moderation queue.

## Review Actions

- **Approve**: Publishes the media item into the destination album.
- **Reject**: Permanently removes the submission from the public gallery.
- **Attribution Review**: Review uploader name, email, submission timestamp, and client source.
