# Mobile App Developer Account Creation Guide

To publish your organization's customized mobile application through the **Google Play Store** and **Apple App Store**, you must create two official developer accounts:

1. **Google Play Console Developer Account** — For Android applications.
2. **Apple Developer Program Account** — For iOS applications.

Both accounts should preferably be registered in the **organization's legal name** rather than an individual's name. These accounts establish your organization's direct legal ownership of its mobile applications on the respective application stores.

Thrico will assist with application configuration, branding, build generation, cryptographic code signing, and store submission.

## 1. Android – Google Play Console Developer Account

Follow these steps to register your organization's Google Play Console developer account.

<Steps>
<Step>
### Step 1: Create a Google Account

If your organization already has an official Google account (e.g., Google Workspace), you can use it. Otherwise, create a dedicated account using your organization's official email address.

- **Website**: [https://accounts.google.com/signup](https://accounts.google.com/signup)

<Callout type="warn">
  **Recommended:** Use an official company email address (such as `developer@yourdomain.com` or `it@yourdomain.com`) rather than a personal Gmail account.
</Callout>
</Step>

<Step>
### Step 2: Register for Google Play Console

Visit the Google Play Console signup portal:

- **Website**: [https://play.google.com/console/signup](https://play.google.com/console/signup)

1. Sign in using your organization's official Google account.
2. Review and accept the **Google Play Developer Distribution Agreement**.
3. Select the appropriate account type (**Organization**).
4. Complete the developer registration form.
5. Pay the one-time registration fee (currently **US$25**, subject to applicable taxes and payment terms).
</Step>

<Step>
### Step 3: Select Account Type (Organization Account)

Always select **Organization Account** (Recommended). Use this option when the application belongs to a registered company, educational institution, association, or eligible corporate entity.

Google will require the following information during registration:
- **Legal organization name** (matching official registration records)
- **Registered business address**
- **Organization contact information** (phone and email)
- **Organization official website**
- **D-U-N-S Number** (Dun & Bradstreet identifier)
- **Identity and organization verification documents**

<Callout type="info">
  Ensure all information submitted exactly matches your organization's official legal records and Dun & Bradstreet listing.
</Callout>
</Step>

<Step>
### Step 4: Complete Organization Verification

Google requires complete organization identity verification before you can publish applications:

- Organization registration documents / certificate of incorporation
- Verified D-U-N-S Number
- Authorized representative identification details
- Identity verification of the account manager
- Official business contact verification (email and SMS OTP verification)

Complete all verification requests directly within the Google Play Console notification banners.
</Step>

<Step>
### Step 5: Provide Access to Thrico

Once the account is created and organization verification is complete, grant access to the Thrico implementation team:

1. Open the [Google Play Console](https://play.google.com/console).
2. Navigate to **Users and permissions** in the left menu.
3. Select **Invite new users**.
4. Enter the email address provided by your Thrico implementation specialist.
5. Assign the permissions required for application setup, release management, and store submission:
   - *App access*: All apps or the specific Thrico-powered application
   - *Permissions*: Release management, store listing creation, and build uploads
6. Click **Send invitation**.

<Callout type="error">
  **Security Warning:** Do **not** share your Google account password or two-step verification codes with Thrico. Always invite Thrico through the official **Users and permissions** delegation panel.
</Callout>

Thrico will use this authorized delegated access to configure, test, sign, and publish your custom-branded Android application.
</Step>
</Steps>

## 2. iOS – Apple Developer Account

Apple requires all custom-branded enterprise applications to be distributed from the client's own Apple Developer Program account.

<Steps>
<Step>
### Step 1: Create an Apple Account

Visit the Apple ID portal:

- **Website**: [https://account.apple.com/](https://account.apple.com/)

1. Select **Create Your Apple Account**.
2. Enter your organization's official work email address.
3. Provide the required personal information for the authorized representative.
4. Verify your email address and phone number.
5. Enable **Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)**.

<Callout type="info">
  The Apple Account must be controlled by an authorized person within your organization who has legal signing authority.
</Callout>
</Step>

<Step>
### Step 2: Enroll in the Apple Developer Program

Visit the enrollment portal:

- **Website**: [https://developer.apple.com/programs/enroll/](https://developer.apple.com/programs/enroll/)

1. Sign in using your organization's Apple Account.
2. Click **Start Your Enrollment**.
3. Select **Organization** as the entity type.
4. Enter your organization's legal entity details.
5. Complete the required identity verification.
</Step>

<Step>
### Step 3: Organization Requirements

Apple requires the following credentials to approve organization enrollment:

| Requirement | Details |
|---|---|
| **Legal Entity Name** | Registered organization name (matching legal records) |
| **D-U-N-S Number** | 9-digit Dun & Bradstreet business identification number |
| **Organization Website** | Official, public, active company website |
| **Work Email** | Organization domain email address (free webmail not accepted) |
| **Legal Authority** | Authorized representative with power to bind the organization legally |
| **Contact Details** | Direct representative contact information |
| **Phone Number** | Verified organization contact phone number |

<Callout type="warn">
  **Important:** Apple organization enrollment requires a registered legal entity (such as LLC, Corp, Ltd, or Inc). A trade name, fictitious business name, DBA, or branch office alone generally does not qualify.
</Callout>
</Step>

<Step>
### Step 4: Obtain or Verify Your D-U-N-S Number

If your organization does not already have a D-U-N-S Number, look up or request one through Apple's designated Dun & Bradstreet lookup tool:

- **Website**: [https://developer.apple.com/enroll/duns-lookup/](https://developer.apple.com/enroll/duns-lookup/)

You must ensure that your organization's legal name, registered address, and jurisdiction match the information registered with Dun & Bradstreet. Please allow several business days for Dun & Bradstreet verification and database syncing to Apple.
</Step>

<Step>
### Step 5: Pay Apple Developer Membership Fee

After Apple completes its review and verification:

1. Return to the [Apple Developer enrollment portal](https://developer.apple.com/programs/enroll/).
2. Review and accept the **Apple Developer Program License Agreement**.
3. Pay the annual membership fee (standard rate: **US$99 per year**, subject to applicable local taxes and regional pricing).
4. Wait for Apple to activate your membership (usually 24–48 hours).
</Step>

<Step>
### Step 6: Provide Access to Thrico via App Store Connect

Once your developer membership is active:

1. Sign in to [App Store Connect](https://appstoreconnect.apple.com/).
2. Navigate to **Users and Access**.
3. Click the **(+) Add User** button.
4. Enter the email address and name provided by your Thrico implementation specialist.
5. Assign appropriate permissions:
   - **Role**: *Admin* or *App Manager*
   - **Developer Resources**: Ensure access to *Certificates, Identifiers & Profiles* is enabled for code signing.
6. Click **Invite**.

<Callout type="error">
  **Security Warning:** The Account Holder should always retain primary ownership of the account. Never share your Apple Account password or two-factor authentication SMS/prompts.
</Callout>
</Step>
</Steps>

## 5. Important Compliance & Security Guidelines

1. **Client Ownership**: Both developer accounts must be owned directly by the client organization. The client retains 100% legal ownership of its developer accounts, publisher identity, and published mobile applications.
2. **Credential Security**: Passwords, 2FA credentials, and SMS verification codes must **never** be shared with Thrico. All collaboration occurs via sanctioned role-based user invitations.
3. **Authorized Access**: Thrico requires invited user permissions to perform application configuration, test releases (TestFlight and Google Play Internal Testing), and production deployments.
4. **Independent Review Policies**: Both Google Play and the Apple App Store operate independent application review processes. Approval timelines are governed solely by Google and Apple policies.
5. **Timeline Expectations**: Organization verification (especially D-U-N-S matching and Apple identity calls) can take between 3 to 10 business days. We recommend initiating this process as early as possible.
6. **Membership Renewals**: Annual Apple Developer Program renewals ($99/year) must be maintained in good standing by the client to ensure continuous app availability and future feature updates.
