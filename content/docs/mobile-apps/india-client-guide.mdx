# India Client Developer Account Setup Guide

| Attribute | Details |
|---|---|
| **Applicable Region** | India |
| **Publishing Platforms** | Google Play Store (Android) & Apple App Store (iOS) |
| **Purpose** | Prepare and verify client developer accounts before Thrico builds and publishes your custom-branded mobile applications |

---

## 1. Overview

To launch your organization's custom-branded Android and iOS mobile applications through Thrico, your company must create and verify two dedicated developer accounts:

1. **Google Play Console** — Android application distribution.
2. **Apple Developer Program** — iOS application distribution.

<Callout type="info">
  **Recommended:** Register both accounts under your Indian legal entity (e.g., Private Limited Company, Public Limited, LLP, registered Trust/Society, or Partnership) using its official legal name as registered with the Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA) and GST records.
</Callout>

---

## 2. Documents and Information Required

Prepare the following documentation before beginning registration:

- **Certificate of Incorporation (COI)** or LLP Agreement / Partnership Deed / Registration Certificate.
- **Company PAN Card** (Permanent Account Number) issued by the Income Tax Department.
- **GST Registration Certificate (GSTIN)** containing your registered business address.
- **Corporate Identification Number (CIN)** or LLPIN (if applicable).
- **Official Registered Business Address** matching your MCA and utility/bank records.
- **Company Website** with a functioning, live public webpage representing your organization.
- **Company-Domain Email Address** (e.g., `developer@yourcompany.in` or `apps@yourcompany.com`).
- **Indian Mobile Number (+91)** for direct verification calls and SMS OTPs.
- **Authorized Signatory / Director KYC Details** (Director Identification Number - DIN, PAN, Aadhaar/Passport).
- **D-U-N-S Number** (Dun & Bradstreet India) or details to apply for one.
- **International Transaction Enabled Credit Card** (Visa, Mastercard, or American Express) compliant with RBI e-mandate guidelines for recurring and international platform charges.

<Callout type="warn">
  All legal names, addresses, and director details submitted must strictly match your MCA/GST records and Dun & Bradstreet listing without abbreviations (e.g., "Private Limited" vs "Pvt Ltd").
</Callout>

## 3. Android Developer Account — Google Play Console

**Registration Portal:** [https://play.google.com/console/signup](https://play.google.com/console/signup)

### Registration Steps

<Steps>
<Step>
### Step 1: Prepare Corporate Google Account
Create or sign in to an official Google Workspace account controlled by your organization (e.g., `developer@yourcompany.in`). Avoid personal Gmail accounts.
</Step>

<Step>
### Step 2: Begin Play Console Registration
Open [Google Play Console Signup](https://play.google.com/console/signup) and sign in with your corporate account.
</Step>

<Step>
### Step 3: Choose Organization Account Type
Select **Organization** as the developer account type. Do not choose Personal.
</Step>

<Step>
### Step 4: Enter Legal Company Details
Enter your legal entity name (exactly matching your Certificate of Incorporation), registered office address, and official contact email/phone.
</Step>

<Step>
### Step 5: Input D-U-N-S Number
Enter your 9-digit D-U-N-S Number. Google will automatically cross-reference Dun & Bradstreet India records to confirm company status.
</Step>

<Step>
### Step 6: Complete Director & Organization Verification
Upload required identity documents:
- Company PAN Card and Certificate of Incorporation (COI) / GST Certificate
- Authorized Director's Government ID (PAN / Passport / Voter ID)
- Proof of business address (recent bank statement or utility bill in company name)
</Step>

<Step>
### Step 7: Verify Domain Ownership
Verify ownership of your company website domain through Google Search Console or DNS TXT records.
</Step>

<Step>
### Step 8: Pay Registration Fee & Activate
Pay the one-time registration fee of **US$25** (approx. ₹2,100 plus applicable taxes/GST). Ensure your card has international transactions enabled in your banking app.
</Step>
</Steps>

### Information to Keep Ready

- Certificate of Incorporation (COI) & Company PAN
- GST Certificate (Form GST REG-06)
- D-U-N-S Number
- Authorized Director/Signatory's ID and contact details
- Company website domain verification access
- Valid international debit/credit card with RBI e-mandate approval

<Callout type="info">
  Google Play Console organization verification for Indian companies typically takes 2–5 business days once all documentation is submitted.
</Callout>

## 4. iOS Developer Account — Apple Developer Program

**Registration Portal:** [https://developer.apple.com/programs/enroll/](https://developer.apple.com/programs/enroll/)

### Registration Steps

<Steps>
<Step>
### Step 1: Create an Apple Account
Create an official Apple Account using the corporate email address of an authorized company representative (e.g., Director or authorized executive).
</Step>

<Step>
### Step 2: Enable Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)
Turn on Two-Factor Authentication on a trusted Apple device (iPhone, iPad, or Mac) or verified mobile number.
</Step>

<Step>
### Step 3: Begin Apple Developer Enrollment
Navigate to [Apple Developer Program Enrollment](https://developer.apple.com/programs/enroll/) and sign in.
</Step>

<Step>
### Step 4: Select Organization Entity Type
Select **Company / Organization** as the enrollment type. Apple requires a registered legal entity with legal binding authority.
</Step>

<Step>
### Step 5: Input Indian Business Details
Enter your company's legal entity name, corporate office address, and D-U-N-S Number.
</Step>

<Step>
### Step 6: Verify Legal Binding Authority
Confirm whether you are the founder/owner or have delegated signing authority to bind the company to legal agreements. Apple will require work email and corporate reference verification.
</Step>

<Step>
### Step 7: Verification Phone Call from Apple
Apple's verification team will call the official corporate telephone number or authorized representative to verify the company's identity and enrollment intent.
</Step>

<Step>
### Step 8: Pay Annual Membership Fee
Once approved by Apple, review the Apple Developer Program License Agreement and pay the annual membership fee of **₹8,700 per year** (or US$99/year plus applicable 18% GST).
</Step>
</Steps>

### Information to Keep Ready

- Certificate of Incorporation (COI) and PAN
- Company D-U-N-S Number
- Official public company website
- Corporate domain email address
- Registered Indian corporate phone number
- Authorized representative's legal title and contact information

<Callout type="warn">
  **RBI Payment Note:** Due to Reserve Bank of India (RBI) e-mandate regulations on recurring subscriptions, use a credit card that supports 3D Secure / OTP authorization for international software purchases.
</Callout>

## 5. D-U-N-S Number Acquisition for Indian Entities

A **D-U-N-S Number** (Data Universal Numbering System) is a 9-digit business identifier issued by Dun & Bradstreet, required by both Apple and Google for organizational identity verification.

If your Indian organization does not already have a D-U-N-S Number:

1. **Search existing listings:** Use Apple's D-U-N-S Lookup tool to search for your company using your registered MCA name.
2. **Check MCA consistency:** Verify that your legal name, registered address, and PIN code match your Certificate of Incorporation and GST certificate.
3. **Request a new D-U-N-S Number:** If not listed, submit a free request via the Apple D&B portal.
4. **Expedited option:** You can also contact Dun & Bradstreet India directly for expedited business verification if on an urgent timeline.

### Official Resources

- **Apple D-U-N-S Lookup Portal**: [https://developer.apple.com/enroll/duns-lookup/](https://developer.apple.com/enroll/duns-lookup/)
- **Google Play Organization Account Requirements**: [https://support.google.com/android-developer-console/answer/16641046](https://support.google.com/android-developer-console/answer/16641046)
- **Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA) Portal**: [https://www.mca.gov.in/](https://www.mca.gov.in/)

<Callout type="info">
  Free D-U-N-S generation through Dun & Bradstreet India typically takes 5–14 business days. Allow an additional 24–48 hours for Apple and Google servers to synchronize the new record.
</Callout>

## 6. Grant Thrico Authorized Access

After both developer accounts are fully activated, invite the designated Thrico implementation team through sanctioned role-based delegation.

<Tabs items={['Google Play Console', 'Apple App Store Connect']}>
  <Tab value="Google Play Console">
    1. Sign in to the [Google Play Console](https://play.google.com/console).
    2. Navigate to **Users and permissions** in the sidebar.
    3. Click **Invite new users**.
    4. Enter the email address provided by your Thrico implementation specialist.
    5. Assign permissions required for application setup, release management, and internal test tracks.
    6. Click **Send invitation**.
  </Tab>
  <Tab value="Apple App Store Connect">
    1. Sign in to [App Store Connect](https://appstoreconnect.apple.com/).
    2. Navigate to **Users and Access**.
    3. Click the **(+)** button to add a new user.
    4. Enter the name and email address provided by your Thrico implementation specialist.
    5. Select the **Admin** or **App Manager** role.
    6. Check **Access to Certificates, Identifiers & Profiles**.
    7. Click **Invite**.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Callout type="error">
  **Security Policy:** The client organization must retain master ownership of both accounts. Account passwords, 2FA prompts, SMS OTPs, and banking credentials must **never** be shared with Thrico.
</Callout>

## 8. Final India Onboarding Checklist

### Google Play Console
- [ ] Corporate Google Workspace account created
- [ ] Organization developer account registered
- [ ] One-time registration fee ($25) paid via international card
- [ ] Company PAN and COI/GST submitted and approved
- [ ] Corporate website domain ownership verified
- [ ] Thrico invited through **Users and permissions**

### Apple Developer Program
- [ ] Corporate Apple Account created with corporate email
- [ ] Two-factor authentication (2FA) active on trusted device
- [ ] D-U-N-S Number matched with MCA records
- [ ] Organization enrollment approved (including Apple phone verification)
- [ ] Annual membership fee paid (₹8,700/year or $99/year + GST)
- [ ] Apple Team ID shared with Thrico
- [ ] Thrico invited through **App Store Connect**
