## Overview

Thrico provides fully customizable, native **Mobile Apps** for both iOS and Android. ## Key Features

- **Custom Organization Branding**: White-labeled with your organization's logo, primary color palette, splash screens, and custom app icons.
- **Native Performance**: Built with high-performance native rendering for fluid gestures, smooth transitions, and low latency.
- **Push Notifications**: Automated push alerts for direct messages, mentions, announcements, event reminders, and community updates via APNs and FCM.
- **Biometric Security**: Support for Face ID, Touch ID, and Android Biometrics for fast, secure member login.
- **Camera & Media Hub**: Direct in-app camera access for uploading photos, videos, and stories to community feeds and media galleries.
- **Offline Caching**: Offline reading mode for recent community feeds, cached directories, and essential discussions.
- **Headless Gamification & Leaderboards**: Live sync with Thrico's enterprise leaderboard and badge rewards infrastructure.

## Publishing Lifecycle

The typical mobile app rollout proceeds in five stages:

1. **Account Registration & Verification**: The client creates and verifies organization developer accounts on Google Play Console and Apple Developer Program.
2. **Access Delegation**: The client invites Thrico's deployment specialists through role-based delegation (no passwords shared).
3. **Branding & Build Configuration**: Thrico configures your entity's branding assets, endpoints, and code signing credentials.
4. **Internal Testing & Review**: Build distribution via TestFlight (iOS) and Google Play Internal Testing for your team to test and sign off.
5. **Store Submission & Approval**: Thrico submits the release for store review, managing the approval cycle through to public launch.
