# UAE Client Developer Account Setup Guide

| Attribute | Details |
|---|---|
| **Applicable Region** | United Arab Emirates (UAE) |
| **Publishing Platforms** | Google Play Store (Android) & Apple App Store (iOS) |
| **Purpose** | Prepare and verify client developer accounts before Thrico builds and publishes your custom-branded mobile applications |

---

## 1. Overview

To launch a custom-branded Android and iOS mobile application through Thrico, your organization must create and verify two dedicated developer accounts:

1. **Google Play Console** — Android application distribution.
2. **Apple Developer Program** — iOS application distribution.

<Callout type="info">
  **Recommended:** Register both accounts under your organization's UAE-registered legal entity using its official legal company name and registered business details as stated on your UAE Trade Licence.
</Callout>

---

## 2. Documents and Information Required

Prepare the following documentation before beginning the registration process:

- **Valid UAE Trade Licence** or equivalent government entity registration decree / free-zone licence (e.g., DED, DIFC, ADGM, DMCC, DSO).
- **Official Legal Company Name** and registered physical address in the UAE.
- **Company Website** with a functioning, live public webpage representing your organization.
- **Company Domain Email Address** (e.g., `admin@yourcompany.ae` or `developer@yourcompany.com`).
- **UAE Telephone Number** and identification details of an authorized representative.
- **D-U-N-S Number** (Dun & Bradstreet identifier), or the information required to apply for one.
- **Valid Payment Method** (corporate credit/debit card) accepted by Google and Apple for regional billing.

<Callout type="warn">
  All legal names, addresses, and representative information submitted must strictly match your official UAE trade licence records and Dun & Bradstreet listing.
</Callout>

## 3. Android Developer Account — Google Play Console

**Registration Portal:** [https://play.google.com/console/signup](https://play.google.com/console/signup)

### Registration Steps

<Steps>
<Step>
### Step 1: Prepare Corporate Google Account
Create or sign in to an official Google account controlled by your organization (e.g., via Google Workspace).
</Step>

<Step>
### Step 2: Begin Play Console Registration
Open [Google Play Console Signup](https://play.google.com/console/signup) and sign in.
</Step>

<Step>
### Step 3: Choose Organization Account
Select **Organization** as the developer account type (do not choose Personal).
</Step>

<Step>
### Step 4: Enter Legal Company Details
Enter your legal company name, UAE registered address, and official corporate contact details.
</Step>

<Step>
### Step 5: Complete Identity Verification
Complete the required identity verification of the authorized account manager.
</Step>

<Step>
### Step 6: Submit D-U-N-S & Trade Licence
Provide your organization's 9-digit D-U-N-S Number and upload your UAE trade licence documents when prompted.
</Step>

<Step>
### Step 7: Verify Domain Ownership
Verify ownership of your company's official website domain using Google Search Console or DNS verification as instructed by Google.
</Step>

<Step>
### Step 8: Pay Registration Fee & Activate
Pay the one-time registration fee of **US$25** (subject to accepted payment methods and applicable local bank conversion charges).
</Step>
</Steps>

### Information to Keep Ready

- UAE Trade Licence (PDF copy)
- Company legal name and registered address (as per trade licence)
- D-U-N-S Number
- Authorized representative's Emirates ID or passport
- Company website URL and access to verify domain ownership
- Company contact email address and UAE telephone number

<Callout type="info">
  Google may request additional documentation depending on your account structure and verification status. Verification typically completes within 2–5 business days.
</Callout>

## 4. iOS Developer Account — Apple Developer Program

**Registration Portal:** [https://developer.apple.com/programs/enroll/](https://developer.apple.com/programs/enroll/)

### Registration Steps

<Steps>
<Step>
### Step 1: Create an Apple Account
Create an official Apple Account (Apple ID) controlled by the authorized company representative.
</Step>

<Step>
### Step 2: Enable Two-Factor Authentication
Enable Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) on the Apple Account using a trusted mobile phone number.
</Step>

<Step>
### Step 3: Begin Apple Developer Enrollment
Navigate to [Apple Developer Program Enrollment](https://developer.apple.com/programs/enroll/) and sign in.
</Step>

<Step>
### Step 4: Select Organization Entity Type
Select **Organization** as the enrollment type.
</Step>

<Step>
### Step 5: Input UAE Business Details
Enter your legal company name and UAE registered business address.
</Step>

<Step>
### Step 6: Provide D-U-N-S Number
Provide your company's verified 9-digit D-U-N-S Number.
</Step>

<Step>
### Step 7: Verify Legal Authority
Verify the organization's legal status and the authorized representative's authority to bind the company to Apple's legal agreements. Apple may call the company's verified telephone number.
</Step>

<Step>
### Step 8: Complete Verification Review
Wait for Apple to review and approve the organization enrollment request.
</Step>

<Step>
### Step 9: Pay Annual Membership Fee
Accept the developer agreement and pay the annual membership fee of **US$99 per year** (or regional UAE equivalent plus applicable VAT/taxes).
</Step>
</Steps>

### Information to Keep Ready

- UAE Trade Licence or supporting legal registration decree
- Company D-U-N-S Number
- Official company website
- Company-domain email address
- UAE business telephone number and contact details
- Authorized representative's personal details and corporate title

<Callout type="warn">
  Apple must approve the organization enrollment before you can proceed to the membership fee payment and account activation.
</Callout>

## 5. D-U-N-S Number Acquisition & Verification

A **D-U-N-S Number** (Data Universal Numbering System) is a unique nine-digit business identifier issued by Dun & Bradstreet, required by both Apple and Google for organizational identity verification.

If your UAE organization does not already have a D-U-N-S Number:

1. **Search existing records:** Search for your company using its registered legal name via Apple's lookup tool.
2. **Verify trade licence details:** Confirm that the registered address and legal entity name in Dun & Bradstreet match your UAE trade licence exactly.
3. **Request a new number:** Submit a request for a free D-U-N-S Number through the Apple D&B portal if one is not found.
4. **Resolve discrepancies:** Correct any variances between your trade licence and Dun & Bradstreet records before continuing the store enrollment.

### Official Resources

- **Apple D-U-N-S Lookup Portal**: [https://developer.apple.com/enroll/duns-lookup/](https://developer.apple.com/enroll/duns-lookup/)
- **Google Play Organization Account Requirements**: [https://support.google.com/android-developer-console/answer/16641046](https://support.google.com/android-developer-console/answer/16641046)

<Callout type="info">
  Allow an additional 3–7 business days for Dun & Bradstreet to generate and sync your D-U-N-S record with Apple and Google databases.
</Callout>

## 6. Grant Thrico Authorized Access

After both developer accounts are fully activated, invite the designated Thrico implementation team to configure and manage your application.

<Tabs items={['Google Play Console', 'Apple App Store Connect']}>
  <Tab value="Google Play Console">
    1. Sign in to the [Google Play Console](https://play.google.com/console).
    2. Navigate to **Users and permissions** in the sidebar.
    3. Select **Invite new users**.
    4. Enter the email address provided by your Thrico implementation specialist.
    5. Assign permissions required for application setup, release management, and store uploads.
    6. Click **Send invitation**.
  </Tab>
  <Tab value="Apple App Store Connect">
    1. Sign in to [App Store Connect](https://appstoreconnect.apple.com/).
    2. Navigate to **Users and Access**.
    3. Click the **(+)** icon to add a new user.
    4. Enter the name and email address provided by your Thrico implementation specialist.
    5. Assign the **Admin** or **App Manager** role.
    6. Ensure access to **Certificates, Identifiers & Profiles** is enabled.
    7. Click **Invite**.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Callout type="error">
  **Security Policy:** The client organization must retain permanent master ownership of both accounts. Developer account passwords, two-factor authentication codes (OTPs), and personal account credentials must **never** be shared.
</Callout>
